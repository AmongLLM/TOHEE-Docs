## 技能

逃亡者属于叛徒阵营，行动方式与普通内鬼相同，可击杀、使用通风口并发动破坏。遗忘者回忆内鬼或叛徒身份、模仿者模仿内鬼、房主将教父的转化结果设为逃亡者，或内鬼阵营玩家死亡后其情人转为逃亡者，都可能产生该职业。

## 选项

- **击杀冷却**：设置逃亡者的击杀冷却。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

The Refugee belongs to the Traitor team and acts like a normal Impostor: it can kill, vent, and sabotage. It can be created when an Amnesiac remembers an Impostor or Madmate, an Imitator imitates an Impostor, the Godfather’s target is killed while the conversion option is set to Refugee, or a Romantic’s Impostor-team partner dies.

## Options

- **Refugee Kill Cooldown**: Kill cooldown for the Refugee.
