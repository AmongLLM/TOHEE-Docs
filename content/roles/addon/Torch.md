## 效果

火炬只分配给船员，视野倍率由主持人设置。默认情况下，灯光破坏不会影响火炬的视野；主持人可启用选项让其受灯光破坏影响。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **TorchVision（火炬视野）**：火炬使用的视野倍率。
- **TorchAffectedByLights（视野受灯光破坏影响）**：控制灯光破坏是否影响火炬的视野。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Torch is assigned only to Crewmates, with a configurable vision multiplier. By default, Lights sabotage does not affect its vision; the host can enable the option to make it affected by Lights sabotage.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **TorchVision**: Vision multiplier used by Torch.
- **TorchAffectedByLights**: Whether Lights sabotage affects Torch's vision.
