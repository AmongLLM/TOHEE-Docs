## 内鬼（Impostor）阵营选项

标准模式（Standard）的内鬼角色页（Impostor Roles）包含阵营级选项以及单个角色配置。当前通用选项包括：

- 是否让内鬼互相看到彼此的角色及附加职业（Add-ons）。
- 内鬼是否能识别和击杀 Madmate。
- Madmate 是否互相识别、识别内鬼、使用内鬼视野或击杀内鬼。
- 内鬼的默认变身冷却，以及死亡后是否仍可破坏。

这些设置会改变队友信息和阵营互动，可能明显改变平衡。调整时先区分“能看到谁”和“能否击杀谁”；单独角色页还会显示各角色的技能选项。具体组合以当前菜单为准。

<!-- en -->
## Impostor faction options

The **Impostor Roles** page in Standard contains faction-wide settings and individual role options. Current general options include:

- Whether Impostors see each other's roles and Add-ons.
- Whether Impostors can identify and kill Madmates.
- Whether Madmates identify one another or Impostors, use Impostor vision, or kill Impostors.
- The default shapeshift cooldown and whether dead Impostors may still sabotage.

These settings change teammate information and faction interactions, so they can significantly affect balance. When tuning them, distinguish “who can be seen” from “who can be killed.” Individual role entries also show each role's ability options. Use the combinations shown in the current menu.
