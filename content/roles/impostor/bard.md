## 技能

吟游诗人存活时，放逐确认会显示由吟游诗人创作的句子，而不是普通确认内容。每次有玩家被放逐后，吟游诗人的击杀冷却会永久减半。

## 出现条件

必须关闭“禁用隐藏职业”，吟游诗人才有机会出现。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

While the Bard is alive, ejection confirmation displays a sentence composed by the Bard instead of the normal confirmation. Each time a player is ejected, the Bard’s kill cooldown is permanently halved.

## Appearance Condition

“Disable Hidden Roles” must be turned off for the Bard to have a chance to appear.
