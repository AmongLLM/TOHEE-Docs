## 效果

幸运加持有一定概率躲过击杀，具体概率由主持人设置。成功躲避时，击杀者会看到护盾动画，但幸运加持自身不会收到提示。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Probability of surviving a kill（击杀存活概率）**：幸运加持躲过击杀的概率。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Lucky has a configurable chance to evade a kill. When it works, the killer sees a shield animation, but Lucky is not notified.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Probability of surviving a kill**: Chance for Lucky to escape a kill.
