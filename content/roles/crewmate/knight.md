## 职业能力

侠客没有任务，整局只能击杀一名玩家。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中侠客的最大人数。
- **击杀冷却**：设置侠客等待多久后才能击杀。
- **可以使用通风口**：开启时侠客可以使用通风口；关闭时不能。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Knight has no tasks and can kill any player only once for the entire game.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Knights in a match.
- **Kill cooldown**: Sets how long the Knight must wait to kill.
- **Can vent**: ON lets the Knight use vents; OFF prevents venting.
