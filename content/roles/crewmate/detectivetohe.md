## 职业能力

侦探（原版）使用游戏原生侦探技能调查线索，与模组自定义的侦探职业不同。

## 设置

- **数量与生成率**：由 TOHE 的角色生成选项控制。
- **嫌疑人数上限**：设置原版侦探技能可涉及的嫌疑人数上限。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The native Detective uses the game's Detective ability to investigate clues. It is separate from the mod's custom Detective role.

## Settings

- **Count and spawn chance**: Controlled by the TOHE role-spawn options.
- **Suspect limit**: Sets the maximum number of suspects for the native Detective ability.
