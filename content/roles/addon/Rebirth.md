## 效果

重生者即将被投出时，会与一名玩家交换外观并继续存活。用尽可重生次数后，会移除重生者附加身份。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Amount of Rebirths（重生次数）**：持有重生者的玩家最多可重生的次数。
- **Only rebirth to players who voted for them（仅重生为投过自己票的玩家）**：控制重生者是否只能变成投票给自己的玩家。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

When about to be exiled, Rebirth swaps skins with another player and survives. Once all rebirths are used, the Rebirth add-on is removed.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Amount of Rebirths**: Maximum number of times the player with Rebirth can rebirth.
- **Only rebirth to players who voted for them**: Whether Rebirth can only become a player who voted for them.
