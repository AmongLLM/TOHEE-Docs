## 瘟疫

瘟疫使者感染所有其他存活玩家后会转变为瘟疫。常规击杀检查中，非天启职业攻击瘟疫会被反弹，攻击者死亡；其他天启职业的攻击会被阻止，但不会反伤。猜测瘟疫职业会使猜测者自杀，而不是杀死瘟疫；投票放逐可以将瘟疫杀死。强制或间接击杀可能绕过常规击杀反弹。瘟疫不必成为最后的幸存者：内鬼全部死亡后，若所有存活的计数中立玩家属于同一计数类型且人数不少于船员，中立阵营即可获胜。

瘟疫转变后的下一次会议中，所有玩家都会得知其存在。

## Pestilence

After the Plaguebearer infects every other living player, they transform into Pestilence. In a standard kill check, an attack by a non-Apocalypse role is reflected and kills the attacker; attacks from other Apocalypse roles are blocked without reflection. Guessing Pestilence's role kills the guesser instead; voting Pestilence out can kill it. Forced or indirect deaths can bypass the standard kill reflection. Pestilence does not need to be the last player alive: after all Impostors are gone, the standard neutral end check awards the win when all living counted neutrals share one count type and their total is at least the Crew count.

Pestilence's presence is announced to everyone at the meeting after the transformation.
