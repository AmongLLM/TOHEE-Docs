## 职业能力

法医可以查看所有玩家的死亡原因，并像科学家一样使用便携生命监测仪。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中法医的最大人数。
- **电池续航时间**：设置法医每次打开生命监测仪可查看多久。
- **所有人都知道法医身份**：开启时公开法医身份；关闭时不公开。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Doctor can see the cause of death for all players and use portable Vitals like a Scientist.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Doctors in a match.
- **Battery duration**: Sets how long the Doctor can keep Vitals open.
- **Everyone knows who the Doctor is**: ON reveals the Doctor; OFF keeps their role secret.
