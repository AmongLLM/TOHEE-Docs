## 效果

每次会议开始时，窃听者都有独立概率看到一条发给特定玩家的随机消息，例如入殓师或侦察员收到的消息。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Chance to eavesdrop（窃听概率）**：看到其他信息类消息的概率。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

At each meeting start, each Eavesdropper has a chance to see one random message sent privately to a player, such as a message received by Mortician or Sleuth.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Chance to eavesdrop**: Chance to see other information-based messages.
