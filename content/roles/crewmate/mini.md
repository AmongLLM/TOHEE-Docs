## 职业能力

迷你船员有好迷你和坏迷你两种变体。详情见[坏迷你船员](/docs/role-evilmini)和[好迷你船员](/docs/role-nicemini)。

## 设置

- **成长所需时间**：设置迷你船员完全长大的秒数。
- **所有人都能看到迷你船员**：开启此项时所有玩家都会知道谁是迷你船员。
- **迷你船员可以是内鬼**：开启时迷你船员可能成为坏迷你船员；关闭时不能成为坏迷你船员。开启后还可设置坏迷你船员的出现概率，以及其未满 18 岁和满 18 岁后的击杀冷却。
- **实时显示年龄变化**：开启时实时显示年龄；关闭时不实时显示。
- **会议时间计入成长**：开启时会议期间也会成长；关闭时会议期间暂停成长。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Mini has two variants, Nice Mini and Evil Mini. See [Evil Mini](/docs/role-evilmini) and [Nice Mini](/docs/role-nicemini) for those rules.

## Settings

- **Time required to grow**: Sets how many seconds it takes the Mini to fully grow up.
- **Everyone can see the Mini**: When enabled, all players know who the Mini is.
- **Mini can be an Impostor**: ON allows an Evil Mini; OFF prevents it. When enabled, settings include the chance of spawning as an Evil Mini and the Evil Mini's kill cooldown while under 18 and over 18.
- **Display age change in real time**: ON displays the Mini's age continuously; OFF does not.
- **Count meeting time toward growth**: ON lets the Mini grow during meetings; OFF pauses growth during meetings.
