## 职业能力

守护天使（TOHE）通过幽灵职业分配规则授予符合条件的死亡玩家，可以为任意阵营的目标施加临时护盾。护盾会在持续时间结束或报告尸体时清除。

## 设置

- **数量与生成率**：由 TOHE 的角色生成选项控制。
- **守护冷却**：设置再次使用保护技能前的等待时间。
- **护盾持续时间**：设置保护效果可持续多久。
- **内鬼可看到护盾**：开启时内鬼能看到守护效果；关闭时看不到。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Guardian Angel (TOHE) is assigned to eligible dead players through the ghost-role assignment rules. It can give a temporary shield to a target of any team. A shield ends when its duration expires or when a body is reported.

## Settings

- **Count and spawn chance**: Controlled by the TOHE role-spawn options.
- **Protect cooldown**: Sets how long the Guardian Angel must wait before protecting again.
- **Protection duration**: Sets how long each shield lasts.
- **Impostors can see protection**: ON lets Impostors see the shield; OFF hides it from them.
