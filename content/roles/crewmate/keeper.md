## 职业能力

守卫者可以在会议中投票给一名玩家，保护该玩家免遭驱逐。可使用的次数由设置限制。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中守卫者的最大人数。
- **保护次数上限**：设置守卫者的技能使用次数。

<!-- en -->
## Role

During a meeting, the Keeper can vote for a player to protect them from being ejected. The number of uses is configurable.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Keepers in a match.
- **Maximum protections**: Sets how many times the Keeper can use the ability.
