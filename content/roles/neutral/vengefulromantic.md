## 复仇浪漫者

当浪漫者的恋人属于船员或不带刀中立并死亡，且能确认凶手时，浪漫者会转变为复仇浪漫者。若无法确认凶手，浪漫者会转为无情浪漫者。复仇浪漫者必须击杀杀死恋人的凶手；成功后击杀按钮会被禁用，两人会在游戏结束时一同获胜。若误杀其他玩家，复仇浪漫者会因误杀而死亡。

## 设置

- **击杀冷却**：再次击杀前的等待时间。
- **可使用通风口**：设置复仇浪漫者能否使用通风口。

## Vengeful Romantic

The Romantic becomes a Vengeful Romantic if their Crewmate or non-killing Neutral partner dies and the killer can be identified. If no killer is recorded, the Romantic becomes a Ruthless Romantic instead. The Vengeful Romantic must kill their partner's killer. After succeeding, the kill button is disabled, and both partners win at the end of the game. Killing anyone else causes a misfire that kills the Vengeful Romantic.

## Settings

- **Kill cooldown:** Time before killing again.
- **Can vent:** Whether the Vengeful Romantic can use vents.
