## 技能

单击击杀键查明目标职业，双击则普通击杀。查明次数用尽后，击杀键只执行普通击杀。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名军师。
- **击杀冷却**：设置击杀冷却。
- **最大查明次数**：设置本局最多可以查明职业的次数。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

Single-click the kill button to reveal a target’s role; double-click to make a normal kill. Once the reveal uses are exhausted, the kill button functions normally.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Consiglieres in a match.
- **Kill Cooldown**: The Consigliere’s kill cooldown.
- **Maximum Reveals**: Maximum number of role reveals in a match.
