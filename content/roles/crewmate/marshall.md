## 职业能力

展现者完成全部任务后会向船员公开身份，包括叛徒等特殊船员阵营。非船员阵营无法看到展现者。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中展现者的最大人数。
- **自定义展现者任务**：开启时可单独设置任务；关闭时与其他玩家任务相同。自定义内容包括是否有公共任务，以及长任务、短任务数量。

<!-- en -->
## Role

After completing all tasks, the Marshall is revealed to the Crew, including special Crewmates such as Madmates. Non-Crew teams cannot see the Marshall.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Marshalls in a match.
- **Override Marshall tasks**: ON allows a separate task setup; OFF gives the Marshall the same tasks as everyone else. The custom setup includes common-task availability and the number of long and short tasks.
