## 效果

静止时，冲刺者的速度会逐渐回升至上限；移动时，速度会逐渐下降至下限。速度调节倍率控制回升和下降的速度。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Min Speed（最低速度）**：冲刺者可达到的最低速度。
- **Max Speed（最高速度）**：冲刺者可达到的最高速度。
- **Speed Modulator（速度调节倍率）**：数值越高，速度恢复和衰减都越快；数值越低，两者都越慢。
- **Display the Charge（显示充能状态）**：控制是否显示冲刺者的速度充能状态。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

While still, Spurt's speed gradually recovers up to its maximum. Moving gradually reduces it to its minimum. The speed modulator controls how quickly speed recovers and falls.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Min Speed**: Minimum speed Spurt can reach.
- **Max Speed**: Maximum speed Spurt can reach.
- **Speed Modulator**: Higher values regenerate and deteriorate speed faster; lower values do both more slowly.
- **Display the Charge**: Whether Spurt's recharge is displayed.
