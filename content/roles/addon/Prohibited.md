## 效果

持有受限者的玩家会遇到部分被封锁、无法使用的通风口。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Override Blocked Vents After Meeting（会议后重设被封锁通风口）**：控制会议结束后是否重设被封锁的通风口。
- **Count Blocked Vents in The Skeld（The Skeld 被封锁通风口数量）**：The Skeld 地图上封锁的通风口数量。
- **Count Blocked Vents in MIRA HQ（MIRA HQ 被封锁通风口数量）**：MIRA HQ 地图上封锁的通风口数量。
- **Count Blocked Vents in Polus（Polus 被封锁通风口数量）**：Polus 地图上封锁的通风口数量。
- **Count Blocked Vents in Dleks（Dleks 被封锁通风口数量）**：Dleks 地图上封锁的通风口数量。
- **Count Blocked Vents in Airship（Airship 被封锁通风口数量）**：Airship 地图上封锁的通风口数量。
- **Count Blocked Vents in The Fungle（The Fungle 被封锁通风口数量）**：The Fungle 地图上封锁的通风口数量。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Some vents are blocked from use for a player with Prohibited.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Override Blocked Vents After Meeting**: Whether blocked vents are overridden after a meeting.
- **Count Blocked Vents in The Skeld**: Number of vents blocked on The Skeld.
- **Count Blocked Vents in MIRA HQ**: Number of vents blocked on MIRA HQ.
- **Count Blocked Vents in Polus**: Number of vents blocked on Polus.
- **Count Blocked Vents in Dleks**: Number of vents blocked on Dleks.
- **Count Blocked Vents in Airship**: Number of vents blocked on Airship.
- **Count Blocked Vents in The Fungle**: Number of vents blocked on The Fungle.
