## 技能

女巫可以直接击杀，也可以施咒。会议中，被施咒目标的名字旁会出现十字标记。若女巫在会议结束前未被放逐或猜中，所有被施咒目标会在会议结束后死亡。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名女巫。
- **切换方式**：选择击杀与施咒的操作方式：
  - **击杀键**：对目标操作时，在普通击杀和施咒之间切换。
  - **通风口**：使用通风口切换普通击杀和施咒。
  - **双击**：单击施咒，双击普通击杀。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

The Witch can make a direct kill or cast a spell. Spelled targets have a cross beside their names during the meeting. If the Witch is not ejected or guessed before the meeting ends, all spelled targets die afterward.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Witches in a match.
- **Switch Action Via**: Choose how to switch between normal kills and spells:
  - **Kill**: Interacting with a target alternates between a normal kill and casting a spell.
  - **Vent**: Interacting with a vent switches between a normal kill and casting a spell.
  - **Double-Click**: Single-click to cast a spell; double-click to make a normal kill.
