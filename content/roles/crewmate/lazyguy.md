## 职业能力

摆烂人只有一个任务，若干内鬼技能不能影响摆烂人，例如傀儡师或术士的控制、匿名者的替罪羊效果。被女巫控制是例外。摆烂人也不能获得附加职业。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中摆烂人的最大人数。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Lazy Guy has one task and is unaffected by certain Impostor abilities, including Puppeteer or Warlock control and Anonymous's scapegoat effect. Being Witched is the exception. The Lazy Guy cannot receive add-ons.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Lazy Guys in a match.
