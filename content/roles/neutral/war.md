## 战争者

狂战士达到房主设定的等级，且开启转变选项后，会转变为战争者。普通击杀检查会被阻止，但战争者仍可被投票放逐；转变后使用战争者的击杀冷却，并保留此前解锁的能力。战争者属于末日阵营，与其他末日角色共同计入胜负。其转变消息会在之后的会议中向所有玩家显示。

冷却、视野、通风口和能力解锁等级等选项位于狂战士设置中。

### 设置

- **转变与等级**：设置是否允许狂战士转变为战争者，以及转变所需的击杀等级。
- **战争者击杀冷却**：转变后使用的击杀冷却时间。
- **战争者视野**：设置战争者是否拥有内鬼视野。
- **战争者可使用通风口**：设置战争者能否使用通风口。
- **解锁能力**：狂战士设置可控制战争者是否获得冷却缩短、食腐者击杀和炸弹能力，以及各能力的解锁等级。

## War

The Berserker transforms into War after reaching the host-configured level when the transformation option is enabled. Ordinary kill checks against War are blocked, but it can still be voted out. After transforming, it uses War's configured kill cooldown and keeps abilities unlocked by the Berserker. War is a Neutral Apocalypse role and counts with the other Apocalypse roles for victory. Its transformation message is shown to everyone at later meetings.

The Berserker settings control War's cooldown, vision, vent access, and ability unlock levels.

### Settings

- **Transformation and level:** Whether Berserker can become War and the required kill level.
- **War kill cooldown:** Cooldown used after transformation.
- **War vision:** Whether War has Impostor vision.
- **War can vent:** Whether War can use vents.
- **Unlocked abilities:** Berserker settings control whether War gains the cooldown reduction, scavenger kill, and bomber abilities and the level required for each.
