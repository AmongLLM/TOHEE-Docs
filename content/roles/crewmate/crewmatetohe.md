## 职业目标

船员的目标是找出并驱逐内鬼。船员阵营通过消灭所有杀手或完成全部任务获胜。

## 设置

- **数量与生成率**：由 TOHE 的角色生成选项控制。

<!-- en -->
## Role objective

The Crewmate's goal is to find and eject the Impostors. The Crew wins by getting rid of all killers or by finishing all tasks.

## Settings

- **Count and spawn chance**: Controlled by the TOHE role-spawn options.
