## 效果

疲劳者尝试使用击杀按钮（包括击杀类能力）或完成任务后，会暂时降低视野和移动速度。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Vision when Tired（疲劳时视野）**：疲劳效果生效时的视野倍率。
- **Speed when Tired（疲劳时速度）**：疲劳效果生效时的移动速度倍率。
- **Tired Duration（疲劳持续时间）**：视野和速度降低的持续时间。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Whenever a Tired player attempts to use a kill button or kill ability, or completes a task, their vision and movement speed temporarily decrease.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Vision when Tired**: Vision multiplier while the Tired effect is active.
- **Speed when Tired**: Movement-speed multiplier while the Tired effect is active.
- **Tired Duration**: How long the reduced vision and speed last.
