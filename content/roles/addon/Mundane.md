## 效果

平凡者只能分配给能够猜测角色的玩家；完成全部任务后才可进行猜测。可分别设置船员和中立者是否能获得此附加身份。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Mundane can only be assigned to players who can guess roles, and they may guess only after completing all tasks. Separate settings control whether Crewmates and Neutrals can receive it.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
