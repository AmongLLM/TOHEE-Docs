## 职业能力

变色龙可以钻通风口暂时隐身。变色龙在自己的屏幕上仍然可见；再次钻通风口会暴露自己。技能有次数限制，完成任务可补充次数。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中变色龙的最大人数。
- **隐身冷却**：设置再次隐身前的等待时间。
- **隐身持续时间**：设置每次隐身可以维持多久。
- **初始技能次数**：设置开局拥有的隐身次数。
- **每完成一个任务获得的技能次数**：设置每完成一个任务增加的技能次数，按 0.1 为步进。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Chameleon can vent to become temporarily invisible. They remain visible on their own screen, and venting again reveals them. Uses are limited and can be replenished by completing tasks.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Chameleons in a match.
- **Disguise cooldown**: Sets how long the Chameleon must wait before disguising again.
- **Disguise duration**: Sets how long each disguise can last.
- **Initial ability uses**: Sets how many disguises the Chameleon starts with.
- **Ability uses gained per task**: Sets the number of uses gained for each completed task, in 0.1 increments.
