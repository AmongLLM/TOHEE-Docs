## 技能

变形后，所有玩家都会显示为外观相同的小黑人。解除变形、隐蔽者死亡或会议开始都会结束伪装。通讯破坏时的伪装效果可以与该技能叠加。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名隐蔽者。
- **伪装冷却**：设置再次伪装前的等待时间。
- **伪装持续时间**：设置伪装持续多久。
- **可以破坏通讯**：开启时可发动通讯破坏；关闭时不能。
- **伪装期间禁止报告尸体**：开启时伪装期间不能报告尸体；关闭时可以报告。
- **显示变形动画**：开启时显示变形动画；关闭时不显示。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

Shapeshifting makes all players appear as identical small black beans. The camouflage ends when the Camouflager unshifts or dies, or when a meeting starts. Comms sabotage camouflage can stack with this ability.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Camouflagers in a match.
- **Camouflage Cooldown**: Time before the ability can be used again.
- **Camouflage Duration**: How long the camouflage lasts.
- **Can Use Comms Sabotage**: When enabled, the Camouflager can sabotage comms; otherwise it cannot.
- **Disable Body Reporting When Camouflage Is Active**: When enabled, bodies cannot be reported during camouflage; otherwise they can.
- **Show Shapeshift Animations**: When enabled, shapeshift animations are shown; otherwise they are hidden.
