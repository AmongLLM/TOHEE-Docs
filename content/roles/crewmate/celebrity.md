## 职业能力

网红死亡时，所有船员都会看到击杀闪光，并在下一次会议收到提示。默认情况下，内鬼和中立阵营不会得知网红死亡；房主可分别设置他们是否能看到击杀闪光。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中网红的最大人数。
- **内鬼知道网红死亡**：开启时内鬼能看到网红死亡时的击杀闪光；关闭时无法判断网红被击杀。
- **中立知道网红死亡**：开启时中立阵营能看到击杀闪光；关闭时无法判断网红被击杀。

<!-- en -->
## Role

When the Celebrity dies, all Crewmates see the kill flash and receive a notice at the next meeting. Impostors and Neutrals do not normally learn of the death, but the host can separately allow each team to see the kill flash.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Celebrities in a match.
- **Impostors know when the Celebrity dies**: ON lets Impostors see the kill flash; OFF prevents them from knowing the Celebrity was killed.
- **Neutrals know when the Celebrity dies**: ON lets Neutrals see the kill flash; OFF prevents them from knowing the Celebrity was killed.
