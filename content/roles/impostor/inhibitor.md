## 技能

重大破坏进行期间不能使用击杀键，例如反应堆、实验室、生命维持系统或直升机破坏；破坏解除后可正常击杀。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名抑郁者。
- **击杀冷却**：设置击杀冷却。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

The Inhibitor cannot use the kill button while a critical sabotage is active, such as Reactor, Laboratory, Life Support, or Helicopter sabotage. It can kill normally after the sabotage is resolved.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Inhibitors in a match.
- **Kill Cooldown**: Time before the Inhibitor can kill again.
