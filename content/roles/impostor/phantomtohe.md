## 技能

使用消失按钮进入隐身状态以躲避击杀；可在持续时间结束前按显形按钮恢复可见。进入和退出隐身都会产生烟雾。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名幻象师。
- **隐身冷却**：设置再次隐身前的等待时间。
- **隐身持续时间**：设置隐身状态持续多久。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

Press the vanish button to become invisible and escape a kill. The Phantom can press the appear button to become visible before the timer ends. A smoke cloud appears both when it vanishes and when it reappears.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Phantoms in a match.
- **Invis Cooldown**: Time before the Phantom can turn invisible again.
- **Invis Duration**: How long the Phantom stays invisible.
