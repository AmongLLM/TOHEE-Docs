## 技能

会议中可查看存活玩家的阵营标记：红色代表内鬼，青色代表船员，灰色代表中立。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名幻想家。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

During meetings, the Visionary sees alignment markers for living players: red for Impostors, cyan for Crewmates, and gray for Neutrals.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Visionaries in a match.
