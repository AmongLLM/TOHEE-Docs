## 效果

最后一名存活的内鬼会获得绝境者效果，其击杀冷却会缩短。

## 设置

- **Kill Cooldown Reduction（击杀冷却缩短比例）**：绝境者的击杀冷却缩短百分比。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

The last surviving Impostor receives this effect, which reduces their kill cooldown.

## Settings

- **Kill Cooldown Reduction**: Percentage by which the Last Impostor's kill cooldown is reduced.
