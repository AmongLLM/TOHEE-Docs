## 职业能力

大明星只有在与杀手单独相处时才能被普通击杀，也不能被职业猜测。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中大明星的最大人数。
- **所有人都知道大明星身份**：开启时在大明星名字旁显示黄色星星；关闭时大明星身份保密。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Super Star can be killed by a regular kill only when alone with a killer. Guessers cannot guess the Super Star.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Super Stars in a match.
- **Everyone knows the Super Star**: ON shows a yellow star next to the Super Star's name; OFF keeps their role secret.
