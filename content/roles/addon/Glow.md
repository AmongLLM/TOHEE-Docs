## 效果

停电期间，光辉自身及其附近玩家都会获得视野加成。光辉不会在 The Fungle 地图随机分配。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Glow Radius（光辉范围）**：光辉对附近玩家生效的距离。
- **Vision Boost for nearby players（附近玩家视野加成）**：附近玩家获得的视野加成幅度。
- **Vision Boost for Glow（Glow 自身视野加成）**：光辉自身获得的视野加成幅度。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

During Lights Out, Glow and nearby players receive a vision boost. Glow is not randomly assigned on The Fungle.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can be in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Glow Radius**: Distance over which Glow affects nearby players.
- **Vision Boost for nearby players**: Strength of the vision boost for nearby players.
- **Vision Boost for Glow**: Strength of the vision boost for Glow itself.
