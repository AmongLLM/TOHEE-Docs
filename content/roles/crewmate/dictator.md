## 职业能力

独裁者投票给一名玩家后，会议会忽略其他所有人的票并立即结束，将独裁者投票的目标驱逐。会议结束时独裁者会自杀。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中独裁者的最大人数。

<!-- en -->
## Role

When the Dictator votes for someone, the meeting ignores everyone else's votes and ends immediately, ejecting the Dictator's target. The Dictator dies as the meeting ends.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Dictators in a match.
