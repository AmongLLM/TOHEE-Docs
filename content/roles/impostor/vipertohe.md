## 技能

毒蛇击杀的尸体会在设定时间后溶解，尸体消失后无法报告。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名毒蛇。
- **尸体溶解时间**：设置击杀尸体多久后溶解。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

Bodies killed by the Viper dissolve after the configured delay and can no longer be reported once gone.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Vipers in a match.
- **Body Dissolve Time**: Time before a killed body dissolves.
