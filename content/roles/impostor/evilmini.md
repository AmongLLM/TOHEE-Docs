## 技能

坏迷你船员是迷你船员的内鬼变体。未满 18 岁时不会被击杀或招募；成长到成年后可以被击杀和招募。其击杀冷却会随年龄增长，从幼年时的较长冷却逐步降低到成年时的较短冷却。

## 选项

- **成长所需时间**：设置迷你船员成长到成年所需时间。
- **所有人都能看到迷你船员**：开启时所有玩家都能识别迷你船员。
- **迷你船员可以成为内鬼**：开启时迷你船员可被分配为坏迷你船员。
  - **坏迷你船员出现概率**：设置迷你船员成为坏迷你船员的概率。
  - **幼年击杀冷却**：设置坏迷你船员未成年时的击杀冷却。
  - **成年击杀冷却**：设置成年后的击杀冷却。
- **实时更新年龄**：开启时实时显示年龄变化。
- **会议期间继续成长**：开启时会议时间也计入成长时间。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

Evil Mini is the Impostor variant of Mini. Before age 18, it cannot be killed or recruited. Once grown up, it can be killed and recruited. Its kill cooldown decreases as it ages, from a longer cooldown while young to a shorter cooldown at adulthood.

## Options

- **Time Required to Grow**: Time for a Mini to reach adulthood.
- **Everyone Can See the Mini**: When enabled, all players can identify Mini.
- **Mini Can Be an Impostor**: When enabled, Mini can be assigned as Evil Mini.
  - **Probability of Mini Being an Impostor**: Chance that Mini becomes Evil Mini.
  - **Kill Cooldown When Under 18**: Evil Mini kill cooldown while young.
  - **Kill Cooldown When Over 18**: Evil Mini kill cooldown after growing up.
- **Display Age Change in Real Time**: When enabled, age changes are shown as they happen.
- **Meeting Time Can Continue to Grow**: When enabled, meeting time counts toward growing up.
