## 跟班

跟班属于豺狼阵营；豺狼获胜时，跟班会一同获胜。豺狼通常在内鬼出局后按人数优势获胜，也可在没有内鬼存活且开启相应选项时通过破坏获胜。豺狼设置中的“跟班计入方式”决定跟班计为豺狼阵营、无阵营或原阵营。

跟班的击杀冷却、通风口和破坏能力由豺狼设置中的跟班选项决定；跟班视野跟随豺狼的内鬼视野设置。豺狼设置还可决定跟班能否击杀豺狼或其他跟班。

## Sidekick

The Sidekick joins the Jackal team and wins when the Jackal team wins. The Jackal normally wins by reaching the endgame count threshold after the Impostors are gone; with the relevant option enabled, the Jackal team can also win by sabotage when no Impostor is alive. The Jackal's Sidekick count mode determines whether Sidekicks count as Jackals, count as no team, or count as their original team.

Jackal settings control the Sidekick's kill cooldown, vent access, and sabotage access; the Sidekick uses the Jackal's Impostor vision setting. Other options determine whether Sidekicks can kill the Jackal or other Sidekicks.
