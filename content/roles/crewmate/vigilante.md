## 职业能力

义务警员负责消灭船员面临的威胁。如果误杀无辜船员，会因内疚和悔恨变成叛徒。歹徒不能将义务警员转换为叛徒。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中义务警员的最大人数。
- **击杀冷却**：设置义务警员再次击杀前的等待时间。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Vigilante is tasked with eliminating threats to the Crew. If they mistakenly kill an innocent Crewmate, they become a Madmate out of guilt and remorse. A Gangster cannot convert the Vigilante into a Madmate.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Vigilantes in a match.
- **Kill cooldown**: Sets how long the Vigilante must wait before killing again.
