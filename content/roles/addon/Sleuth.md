## 效果

侦察员可以从尸体获得信息，并可由设置决定是否查明击杀者的角色。它与胆小鬼、侦探、入殓师、清理工、美杜莎、秃鹫和验尸官不兼容。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Can find the role of the killer（可查明击杀者角色）**：控制侦察员能否查明击杀者的角色。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Sleuth can gain information from dead bodies, and settings can allow it to learn the killer's role. It is incompatible with Oblivious, Detective, Mortician, Cleaner, Medusa, Vulture, and Coroner.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Can find the role of the killer**: Whether Sleuth can find the killer's role.
