## 技能

使用击杀键选择一名玩家作为阴，再选择另一名玩家作为阳。两人相遇时会互相击杀。阴阳都选定后，阴阳命煞人可以正常击杀；会议开始会清除已选的阴阳目标。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名阴阳命煞人。
- **击杀冷却**：设置两次选择或击杀之间的冷却时间。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

Use the kill button once to choose a player as Yin and a second time to choose a player as Yang. When the two meet, they kill each other. Once both are selected, the YinYanger can make normal kills; a meeting clears the selected Yin and Yang.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of YinYangers in a match.
- **Kill Cooldown**: Time before the YinYanger can select or kill again.
