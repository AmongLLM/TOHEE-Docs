## 职业能力

鬼怪可以附身一名毫无防备的玩家，再为其选择一个目标。在附身期间，该玩家只能对指定目标使用击杀或击杀类技能；鬼怪改为附身其他玩家、效果到期，或附身者/指定目标死亡时，此限制解除。

## 设置

- **附身冷却**：设置鬼怪再次附身前的等待时间。
- **附身次数上限**：设置鬼怪最多可附身的次数。
- **附身持续时间**：设置附身效果持续多久。
- **鬼怪速度**：设置鬼怪的移动速度。
- **不能附身队友**：开启时不能附身盟友；关闭时可以。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Ghastly can possess an unsuspecting player and then choose a target for them. While possessed, that player can use their kill or kill ability only on the chosen target. The restriction ends when the Ghastly possesses someone else, the effect expires, or either the possessed player or chosen target dies.

## Settings

- **Possess cooldown**: Sets how long the Ghastly must wait before possessing again.
- **Maximum possessions**: Sets how many times the Ghastly can possess players.
- **Possession duration**: Sets how long each possession lasts.
- **Ghastly speed**: Sets how fast the Ghastly can move.
- **Cannot possess allies**: ON prevents possessing allies; OFF allows it.
