## 效果

梦魇只分配给内鬼。灯光破坏生效时，梦魇获得额外移动速度和更短的击杀冷却，并会以阵营颜色显示名字；灯光恢复后，击杀按钮不可用。梦魇不会在 The Fungle 地图随机分配。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **MareAddSpeedInLightsOut（灯光破坏期间额外速度）**：灯光破坏期间增加的移动速度。
- **MareKillCooldownInLightsOut（灯光破坏期间击杀冷却）**：灯光破坏期间的击杀冷却。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Mare is assigned only to Impostors. During Lights Out, it gains additional movement speed and a shorter kill cooldown, and its name is shown in its team color. Its kill button is unavailable outside Lights Out. Mare is not randomly assigned on The Fungle.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **MareAddSpeedInLightsOut**: Additional movement speed during Lights Out.
- **MareKillCooldownInLightsOut**: Kill cooldown during Lights Out.
