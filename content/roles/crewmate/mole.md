## 职业能力

鼹鼠钻入通风口后会在其中停留一秒。离开时会出现在地图上另一个随机通风口旁，不会回到刚使用的那个通风口。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中鼹鼠的最大人数。
- **挖掘冷却**：设置鼹鼠再次使用能力前的等待时间。

<!-- en -->
## Role

When the Mole vents, they stay in the vent for one second. When they come out, they appear near a random vent on the map other than the one they just used.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Moles in a match.
- **Dig cooldown**: Sets how long the Mole must wait before using the ability again.
