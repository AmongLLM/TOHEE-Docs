## 职业能力

好迷你船员长大前不会被击杀。如果其在长大前死亡或在会议中被驱逐，游戏会以好迷你为胜者结束。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Nice Mini cannot be killed until they grow up. If they die or are ejected from a meeting before growing up, the game ends with the Nice Mini as the winner.
