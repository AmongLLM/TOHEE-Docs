## 效果

逃避者被投票出局时，有概率不会被驱逐。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Max Ability Uses（能力使用次数上限）**：逃避者可逃脱驱逐的次数上限。
- **Chance to not be exiled（免于驱逐概率）**：逃避者逃脱驱逐的概率。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

When Evader is voted out, there is a chance they will not be exiled.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Max Ability Uses**: Maximum number of times Evader can escape exile.
- **Chance to not be exiled**: Chance for Evader to escape exile.
