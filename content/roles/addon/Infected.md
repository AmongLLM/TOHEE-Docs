## 效果

感染者可将符合条件的船员、内鬼或中立击杀者转化为感染。感染与感染者同属感染阵营并一同获胜；若感染者被投出，所有存活的感染都会死亡。此身份不会独立随机分配。

## 相关设置

- **Infectious bite settings（感染者咬合设置）**：感染者设置控制咬合次数、阵营成员的互相识别、通风口与视野，以及是否启用双击击杀。
- **随机分配**：不会开局随机分配；由上述赋予者能力转化获得。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Infectious can convert eligible Crewmates, Impostors, or Neutral Killers into Infected. Infected players share Infectious's team and win together; if Infectious is exiled, all living Infected players die. This identity is not independently assigned at random.

## Related settings

- **Infectious bite settings**: Infectious settings control bite uses, who knows the infected players, ventilation and vision, and whether double-click killing is enabled.
- **Random assignment**: Not assigned at random at game start; it is granted by the conversion ability described above.
