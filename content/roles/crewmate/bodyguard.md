## 职业能力

如果附近玩家即将被击杀，保镖会代替目标挡下攻击，并与凶手同归于尽。保镖可以保护任何阵营；成为叛徒时，若凶手是内鬼，保镖不会触发保护。该技能不拦截任务大师、十字军、老兵或捕快的击杀互动。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中保镖的最大人数。
- **保护范围**：设置目标必须距离保镖多近，保镖才能触发保护。

<!-- en -->
## Role

If a player near the Bodyguard is about to be killed, the Bodyguard prevents the attack and dies with the murderer. The Bodyguard can protect players of any team. As a Madmate, the Bodyguard does not activate the skill against an Impostor. The protection does not intercept interactions from Taskinator, Crusader, Veteran, or Deputy.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Bodyguards in a match.
- **Protect radius**: Sets how close the Bodyguard must be to the target to trigger the protection.
