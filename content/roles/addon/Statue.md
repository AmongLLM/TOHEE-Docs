## 效果

当有足够多玩家靠近雕像时，雕像会依主持人设置减速或完全无法移动。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Statue Slowness（雕像减速比例）**：雕像被减速的幅度。
- **People needed to Slow（触发减速所需人数）**：触发雕像减速所需的附近玩家人数。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

When enough players are near a Statue, it is slowed or completely frozen depending on host settings.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Statue Slowness**: Amount by which the Statue is slowed.
- **People needed to Slow**: Number of nearby players required to slow the Statue.
