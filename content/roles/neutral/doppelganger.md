## 替身者

常规职业分配中，替身者的人数上限固定为一名。替身者使用击杀按钮窃取目标的名字和外观，并击杀目标。目标正在变形、伪装或蘑菇混乱破坏生效时，以及窃取次数用尽后，击杀不会交换身份。内鬼阵营消失后，若替身者是唯一存活的计数中立阵营且人数不少于船员，即可获胜。

### 设置

- **最大身份窃取次数**：替身者可窃取其他玩家身份的次数。
- **击杀冷却**：再次击杀前的等待时间。
- **可使用通风口**：设置替身者能否使用通风口。
- **内鬼视野**：设置替身者是否拥有内鬼视野。

## Doppelganger

Normal role assignment has a fixed limit of one Doppelganger. The Doppelganger uses the kill button to steal a target's name and appearance, then kills the target. They do not swap identities if the target is shapeshifting, Camouflage or Mushroom Mixup is active, or the steal limit has been reached. After Impostors are gone, Doppelgangers win when they are the only surviving counted Neutral team and at least as numerous as Crew.

### Settings

- **Maximum steals:** Number of player identities the Doppelganger can steal.
- **Kill cooldown:** Time before killing again.
- **Can vent:** Whether the Doppelganger can use vents.
- **Impostor vision:** Whether the Doppelganger has Impostor vision.
