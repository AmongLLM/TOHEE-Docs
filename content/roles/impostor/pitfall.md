## 技能

变形会在周围区域设置陷阱。进入陷阱的玩家会短暂无法移动，视野也会受到影响。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名设陷者。
- **陷阱冷却**：设置再次布置陷阱前的等待时间。
- **陷阱数量**：设置可同时布置的陷阱数量。
- **每个陷阱可捕获人数**：设置单个陷阱最多可困住多少名玩家。
- **陷阱持续时间**：设置陷阱保持有效多久。
- **陷阱半径**：设置陷阱范围大小。
- **冻结时间**：设置被困玩家无法移动多久。
- **视野影响程度**：设置陷阱对玩家视野的影响。
- **视野影响时间**：设置视野影响持续多久。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

Shapeshifting places a trap in the surrounding area. Players who enter it are immobilized briefly and have their vision affected.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Pitfalls in a match.
- **Trap Cooldown**: Time before the Pitfall can place another trap.
- **Number of Traps**: Maximum number of traps that can be placed.
- **Players Caught per Trap**: Maximum number of players a single trap can catch.
- **Trap Active Time**: How long a trap remains active.
- **Trap Radius**: Size of the trap area.
- **Trap Freeze Time**: How long caught players are immobilized.
- **Trap Vision Effect**: Amount of vision impairment caused by the trap.
- **Trap Vision Duration**: How long the vision effect lasts.
