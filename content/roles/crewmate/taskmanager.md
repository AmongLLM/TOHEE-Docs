## 职业能力

任务管理者会在职业名称旁看到全体玩家已完成任务的总数，并实时更新。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中任务管理者的最大人数。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Task Manager sees the total number of tasks completed by everyone together beside their role name. The count updates in real time.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Task Managers in a match.
