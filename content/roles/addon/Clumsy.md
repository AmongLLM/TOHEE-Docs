## 效果

笨蛋有概率无法成功击杀目标。击杀落空时，目标不受影响，且笨蛋的击杀冷却会重置。它只分配给内鬼阵营角色。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Chance to miss a kill（击杀落空概率）**：笨蛋击杀落空的概率。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Clumsy has a chance to miss a kill. On a miss, the target remains untouched and the cooldown resets. It is assigned only to Impostor-team roles.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Chance to miss a kill**: Chance for Clumsy to miss a kill.
