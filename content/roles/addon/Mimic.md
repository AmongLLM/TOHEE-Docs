## 效果

只有内鬼可以成为宝箱怪。宝箱怪死亡后，在下一次会议中，内鬼队伍会收到它所击杀且目前仍存活的玩家角色信息。存活的宝箱怪还可按设置查看已死亡玩家的角色。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Mimic can see the roles of dead players（Mimic 可查看死者角色）**：控制存活的宝箱怪能否在角色可见后查看死者身份。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Only Impostors can become Mimic. At the next meeting after Mimic dies, the Impostor team receives the roles of players it killed who are still alive. While alive, Mimic can also see dead players' roles if the setting allows it.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Mimic can see the roles of dead players**: Whether a living Mimic can see dead players' roles when roles are visible.
