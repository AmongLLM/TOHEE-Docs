## 效果

倒霉蛋执行特定动作时可能自杀。主持人可分别设置尝试击杀、完成任务、使用通风口、报告尸体和开门时触发自杀的概率。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Chance to suicide from killing（尝试击杀时自杀概率）**：尝试击杀时自杀的概率。
- **Chance to suicide from doing tasks（做任务时自杀概率）**：完成任务后自杀的概率。
- **Chance to suicide from venting（使用通风口时自杀概率）**：使用通风口后自杀的概率。
- **Chance to suicide from reporting bodies（报告尸体时自杀概率）**：报告尸体后自杀的概率。
- **Chance to suicide from opening a door（开门时自杀概率）**：开门后自杀的概率。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Unlucky can die by suicide when performing certain actions. The host can separately configure the chance for attempting a kill, completing tasks, venting, reporting bodies, and opening doors.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Chance to suicide from killing**: Chance to die by suicide when attempting a kill.
- **Chance to suicide from doing tasks**: Chance to die by suicide after doing a task.
- **Chance to suicide from venting**: Chance to die by suicide after venting.
- **Chance to suicide from reporting bodies**: Chance to die by suicide after reporting a body.
- **Chance to suicide from opening a door**: Chance to die by suicide after opening a door.
