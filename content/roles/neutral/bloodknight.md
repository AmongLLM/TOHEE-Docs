## 嗜血骑士

内鬼阵营消失后，若嗜血骑士是唯一存活的计数中立杀手阵营，且嗜血骑士人数不少于船员人数，嗜血骑士阵营即可获胜。每次击杀后，嗜血骑士都会获得一段时间的护盾，在此期间不会死亡。

## 设置

- **击杀冷却**：再次击杀前的等待时间。
- **可使用通风口**：设置嗜血骑士能否使用通风口。
- **内鬼视野**：开启后拥有内鬼视野；关闭后使用默认视野。
- **保护持续时间**：击杀后无敌护盾持续的时间。

## Blood Knight

After Impostors are gone, the Blood Knight wins when it is the only surviving counted Neutral team and is at least as numerous as Crew. After each kill, the Blood Knight gains a temporary shield that makes them invulnerable for a few seconds.

## Settings

- **Kill cooldown:** Time before the Blood Knight can kill again.
- **Can vent:** Whether the Blood Knight can use vents.
- **Impostor vision:** When enabled, the Blood Knight has Impostor vision; otherwise, they have default vision.
- **Protection duration:** How long the post-kill shield lasts.
