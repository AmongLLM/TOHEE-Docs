## 职业能力

十字军使用击杀按钮为一名玩家施加保护。多数攻击者会被十字军击杀；任务执行者、保镖、老兵和捕快不会触发这次反击。若攻击者是瘟疫，十字军会死亡，受保护的玩家则挡下攻击。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中十字军的最大人数。
- **十字军冷却**：设置再次使用技能前的等待时间。
- **十字军次数上限**：设置一局中十字军最多可以使用技能的次数。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Crusader uses the kill button to protect a player. Most attackers are killed by the Crusader, but attacks by Taskinator, Bodyguard, Veteran, or Deputy do not trigger retaliation. If the attacker is Pestilence, the Crusader dies and the protected player blocks the attack.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Crusaders in a match.
- **Crusade cooldown**: Sets how long the Crusader must wait before using the ability again.
- **Maximum crusades**: Sets how many times the Crusader can use the ability in one game.
