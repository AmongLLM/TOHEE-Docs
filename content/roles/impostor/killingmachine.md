## 技能

杀戮机器的击杀冷却很短，视野与船员相同。它不能使用通风口、发动破坏、报告尸体或召开紧急会议。击杀会无视所有护盾；击杀诱饵或诡雷不会触发这些角色的特殊效果。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少台杀戮机器。
- **击杀冷却**：设置击杀冷却。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

The Killing Machine has a very short kill cooldown and the same vision as Crewmates. It cannot vent, sabotage, report bodies, or call emergency meetings. Its kills bypass all shields. Killing a Bait or Trapster does not trigger those roles’ special effects.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Killing Machines in a match.
- **Kill Cooldown**: The Killing Machine’s kill cooldown.
