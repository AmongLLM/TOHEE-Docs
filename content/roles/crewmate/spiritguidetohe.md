## 职业能力

死亡后，灵媒可使用原版灵媒技能向存活玩家发送图像消息；通讯破坏期间无法使用技能。

## 设置

- **数量与生成率**：由 TOHE 的幽灵职业生成设置控制。
- **技能冷却**：设置再次发送图像消息前的等待时间。

<!-- en -->
## Role

After death, the Spirit Guide uses the native Spirit Guide ability to send image messages to living players. Communications sabotage prevents the ability from being used.

## Settings

- **Count and spawn chance**: Controlled by the mod's ghost-role settings.
- **Ability cooldown**: Sets how long the Spirit Guide must wait before sending another image message.
