## 效果

持枪只会分配给拥有击杀按钮的角色，并使其获得游戏中最长的击杀距离。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Reach is assigned only to roles with a kill button and gives them the longest kill range possible in the game.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
