## 职业能力

侦探报告尸体后会收到线索，得知死者的职业；根据设置，也可能得知凶手的职业。侦探不会成为 Oblivious。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中侦探的最大人数。
- **可以查出凶手职业**：开启时，侦探可得知其报告的尸体对应凶手的职业；关闭时无法得知凶手的确切职业。

<!-- en -->
## Role

After reporting a body, the Detective receives a clue identifying the dead player's role and may learn the killer's role, depending on settings. The Detective cannot become Oblivious.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Detectives in a match.
- **Can find the killer's role**: ON lets the Detective learn the role of the killer associated with a reported body; OFF withholds the killer's exact role.
