## 职业能力

瞭望员存活时，其他仍存活玩家的 ID 会显示在名称旁；瞭望员死亡后或目标死亡后不再显示。若模组的玩家 ID 功能负责显示，则改由该功能处理。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中瞭望员的最大人数。

<!-- en -->
## Role

While the Lookout is alive, IDs appear beside the names of other living players. They are not shown for a dead Lookout or a dead target; if the mod's Player Identifiers feature manages IDs for the seer, that feature handles the display instead.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Lookouts in a match.
