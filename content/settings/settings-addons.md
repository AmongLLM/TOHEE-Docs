## 通用附加职业（Add-ons）选项

附加职业（Add-ons）是叠加在角色上的修饰效果。打开 **Addons** 菜单后，可以查看每项附加职业的数量、出现几率和角色限制。当前通用选项包括：

- **在名字旁显示附加职业：** 控制玩家名字旁是否显示附加职业。关闭后不会取消效果，只是隐藏显示。
- **括号和短名称：** 调整显示样式，便于将多个角色修饰与角色名称区分开。
- **附加职业数量上限：** 默认限制较低；增大数量后玩家可能获得多个修饰。先考虑效果冲突和信息负担，再提高上限。

部分附加职业只允许特定阵营或角色获得，部分会与其他附加职业冲突。每项附加职业下的选项说明决定它的具体效果；旧上游页面的分类或总数可能与当前版本不同。

<!-- en -->
## General Add-on options

Add-ons are modifiers layered on top of roles. Open the **Addons** menu to see each Add-on's count, spawn chance, and role restrictions. Current general options include:

- **Display Add-ons by the name:** Controls whether Add-ons appear beside player names. Turning this off hides the display; it does not remove the effect.
- **Brackets and short names:** Adjust the display style so multiple modifiers are easier to distinguish from the role name.
- **Maximum Add-on count:** The default limit is low; raising it can give players multiple modifiers. Consider conflicting effects and information load before increasing the limit.

Some Add-ons are limited to certain factions or roles, and some conflict with other Add-ons. The description under each Add-on controls its specific behavior; categories and total counts on old upstream pages may differ from the current release.
