## 技能

击杀时，当前房间内的玩家会短暂失明；会议开始会结束失明效果。

## 选项

- **致盲时忽略内鬼**：开启时，其他内鬼不会被致盲；关闭时，其他内鬼也会失明。
- **致盲持续时间**：设置失明效果持续多久。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

When the Stealth kills, players in the same room are blinded briefly. A meeting ends the blindness effect.

## Options

- **Ignore Impostors While Blinding**: When enabled, other Impostors are not blinded; when disabled, they are blinded too.
- **Blinding Duration**: How long players remain blinded.
