## 职业能力

竞猜大师会收到会议中每次职业猜测的信息，包括猜测者试图猜测的职业；若猜测错误，也会收到通知。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中竞猜大师的最大人数。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Guess Master receives information about every attempted role guess during a meeting. They learn which role the guesser tried to guess and are notified if the guess was wrong.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Guess Masters in a match.
