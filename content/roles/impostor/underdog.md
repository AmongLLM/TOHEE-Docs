## 技能

当存活玩家人数不多于房主设定的门槛时，失败者才能使用击杀键。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名失败者。
- **开始击杀所需的玩家数量上限**：设置失败者可开始击杀时的存活人数门槛。
- **击杀冷却**：设置击杀冷却。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

The Underdog can use the kill button only when the number of living players is at or below the host-set threshold.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Underdogs in a match.
- **Maximum Players Needed to Start Killing**: Living-player threshold at which the Underdog can kill.
- **Kill Cooldown**: Time before the Underdog can kill again.
