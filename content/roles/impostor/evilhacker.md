## 技能

每次会议开始时获得最后一刻的管理室信息。没有人的房间不会显示；带「★」的房间表示有内鬼，房间后面的数字表示尸体数量，例如「★餐厅：3（尸体×1）」。

## 选项

- **查看尸体位置**：开启后可查看尸体所在位置。
- **查看其他内鬼位置**：开启后可查看其他内鬼的位置。
- **查看击杀闪光**：开启后可像灵媒一样看到击杀闪光；可进一步设置是否显示击杀地点。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

At the beginning of each meeting, the Evil Hacker receives the most recent Admin information. Unoccupied rooms are omitted. A 「★」 marks a room with an Impostor; the number after a room indicates its body count, for example, 「★Cafeteria: 3 (DEAD×1)」.

## Options

- **Can See the Location of Dead Bodies**: When enabled, shows where bodies are located.
- **Can See the Location of Other Impostors**: When enabled, shows where other Impostors are located.
- **Can See Kill-Flash**: When enabled, shows kill flashes like Seer; a nested option controls whether the murder location is also shown.
