## 独行者

独行者可以像连环杀手一样击杀玩家，但会随最终获胜阵营获胜。独行者存活到游戏结束且达到最低击杀数后，才会获得额外胜利；死亡则不能获胜。

### 设置

- **击杀冷却**：再次击杀前的等待时间。
- **可使用通风口**：设置独行者能否使用通风口。
- **内鬼视野**：开启后拥有内鬼视野；关闭后使用默认视野。
- **获胜所需最低击杀数**：独行者获得额外胜利所需的击杀数；设为 0 时不要求击杀数。

<!-- en -->
## Maverick

The Maverick can kill like a Serial Killer and wins with the winning team. To receive the Maverick win, they must survive until the end and meet the minimum kill count. They cannot win if they die.

### Settings

- **Kill cooldown:** Time before the Maverick can kill again.
- **Can vent:** Whether the Maverick can use vents.
- **Impostor vision:** When enabled, the Maverick has Impostor vision; otherwise, they have default vision.
- **Minimum kills to win:** Number of kills needed for the Maverick win; zero disables the requirement.
