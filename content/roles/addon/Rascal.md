## 效果

流氓只分配给船员。警长可以击杀流氓；若告密者设置为可查找叛徒，也能找到流氓。被驱逐时是否像叛徒一样显示由选项控制。商人可以出售该有害附加身份给符合条件的船员。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **RascalAppearAsMadmate（被驱逐时显示为 Madmate）**：控制流氓被驱逐时是否像叛徒一样显示。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Rascal is assigned only to Crewmates. Sheriff can kill a Rascal, and Snitch can find one if its setting allows it to find Madmates. A setting controls whether Rascal appears as a Madmate when ejected. Merchant can also sell this harmful add-on to an eligible Crewmate.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **RascalAppearAsMadmate**: Whether Rascal appears the same way as a Madmate when ejected.
