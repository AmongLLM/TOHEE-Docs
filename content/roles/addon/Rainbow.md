## 效果

彩虹会不断快速改变颜色。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Eligibility（可分配阵营）**：主持人可分别设置内鬼、船员、中立者是否可以获得此附加身份。
- **Cooldown for changing colors（换色冷却）**：彩虹改变颜色的间隔。
- **Rainbow color changes during Camouflage（伪装期间继续换色）**：控制伪装效果生效时彩虹是否继续改变颜色。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Rainbow changes colors repeatedly.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Eligibility**: Separate settings control whether Impostors, Crewmates, Neutrals can receive this add-on.
- **Cooldown for changing colors**: How often Rainbow changes colors.
- **Rainbow color changes during Camouflage**: Whether Rainbow continues changing colors while Camouflage is active.
