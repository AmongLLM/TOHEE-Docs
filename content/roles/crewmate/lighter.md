## 职业能力

执灯人可以通过钻通风口暂时提高自己的视野。正常照明和停电时都会获得增强视野。

## 设置

- **点灯冷却**：设置再次使用技能前的等待时间。
- **点灯持续时间**：设置视野增强持续多久。
- **正常照明时的视野增强**：设置普通情况下增加的视野。
- **停电时的视野增强**：设置停电期间增加的视野。
- **初始技能次数**：设置执灯人开局拥有的技能次数。
- **每完成一个任务获得的技能次数**：设置每完成一个任务增加的次数，按 0.1 为步进。

<!-- en -->
## Role

The Lighter can vent to temporarily increase their vision. The increased vision applies both when lights are on and when lights are out.

## Settings

- **Light cooldown**: Sets how long the Lighter must wait before using the ability again.
- **Light duration**: Sets how long the vision increase lasts.
- **Increased vision**: Sets the vision increase under normal lighting.
- **Increased vision during lights out**: Sets the vision increase during a lights sabotage.
- **Initial ability uses**: Sets how many uses the Lighter starts with.
- **Ability uses gained per task**: Sets the uses gained for each completed task, in 0.1 increments.
