## 技能

只要教唆者仍存活，每当会议投票放逐一名无辜船员，投票给该玩家的额外船员会在会议结束后死亡。额外死亡人数由房主设置。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名教唆者。
- **击杀冷却**：设置击杀冷却。
- **技能使用次数**：设置可使用技能的次数。
- **每次技能击杀人数**：设置每次技能使用可造成的击杀人数。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

While the Instigator is alive, whenever an innocent Crewmate is voted out, additional Crewmates who voted for that player die after the meeting. The host sets how many additional players die.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Instigators in a match.
- **Kill Cooldown**: Time before the Instigator can kill again.
- **Ability Use Count**: Number of times the ability can be used.
- **Kills per Ability Use**: Number of kills caused by each use.
