## 技能

变形为其他玩家。变形和解除变形时的效果会很明显；可设置是否留下变形痕迹。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名变形者。
- **变形冷却**：设置再次变形前的等待时间。
- **变形持续时间**：设置变形状态持续多久。
- **留下变形痕迹**：开启时会留下变形证据；关闭时不会。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

Shapeshift into another player. The shapeshift and revert are noticeable; a setting controls whether evidence is left behind.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Shapeshifters in a match.
- **Shapeshift Cooldown**: Time before shapeshifting again.
- **Shapeshift Duration**: How long the disguise lasts.
- **Leave Shapeshifting Evidence**: When enabled, shapeshifting leaves evidence; otherwise it does not.
