## 技能

野人可以变形但不能进入通风口。每次击杀后会获得一段时间的无敌保护。

## 选项

- **击杀后保护时间**：设置击杀后无敌状态持续多久。
- **变形冷却**：设置再次变形前的等待时间。
- **变形持续时间**：设置每次变形可持续多久。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

The Wildling can shapeshift but cannot vent. After a kill, it gains invulnerability for a limited time.

## Options

- **Protection Duration**: How long invulnerability lasts after a kill.
- **Shapeshift Cooldown**: Time before the Wildling can shapeshift again.
- **Shapeshift Duration**: How long a shapeshift can last.
