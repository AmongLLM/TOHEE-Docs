## 效果

超频波会按设定比例缩短玩家的击杀冷却时间，只分配给拥有击杀按钮的角色。

## 设置

- **Max（最大数量）**：本局中该附加身份可出现的数量上限。
- **Spawn Chance（生成概率）**：玩家获得此附加身份的概率。
- **Kill Cooldown Reduction（击杀冷却缩短比例）**：玩家击杀冷却缩短的百分比。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Overclocked reduces a player's kill cooldown by a configured percentage and is assigned only to roles with a kill button.

## Settings

- **Max**: Maximum number that can appear in one match.
- **Spawn Chance**: Chance for a player to receive this add-on.
- **Kill Cooldown Reduction**: Percentage by which the player's kill cooldown is reduced.
