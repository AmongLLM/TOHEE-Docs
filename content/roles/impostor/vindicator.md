## 技能

卫道士在会议投票时额外投出若干票，效果类似市长。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名卫道士。
- **额外票数**：设置卫道士额外获得的票数。
- **隐藏额外票**：开启时额外票不显示，但仍计入票数；关闭时额外票正常显示。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

The Vindicator casts extra votes during meetings, like a Mayor.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Vindicators in a match.
- **Additional Votes Count**: Number of extra votes the Vindicator receives.
- **Hide Additional Votes**: When enabled, extra votes are not shown but still count; otherwise they appear as normal votes.
