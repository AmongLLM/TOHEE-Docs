## 职业能力

守护者完成全部任务后不会被击杀，也不能被猜测为守护者。该效果不阻止会议驱逐。

## 设置

- **数量上限**：设置一局中守护者的最大人数。
- **自定义守护者任务**：开启时可单独设置任务；关闭时守护者与其他玩家任务相同。自定义内容包括是否有公共任务，以及长任务、短任务数量。

<!-- en -->
## Role

After completing all tasks, the Guardian cannot be killed or guessed as the Guardian. This does not prevent ejection.

## Settings

- **Maximum count**: Sets the maximum number of Guardians in a match.
- **Override Guardian tasks**: ON allows a separate task setup; OFF gives the Guardian the same tasks as everyone else. The custom setup includes common-task availability and the number of long and short tasks.
