## 技能

变形期间使用绞杀击杀。绞杀无视目标状态，例如医护的护盾、保镖或超级巨星的技能；不会留下尸体，也不会触发受害者技能（例如老兵反击、幸运儿存活），灵媒也不会收到提示。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名刽子手。
- **变形冷却**：设置再次变形并使用绞杀前的等待时间。
- **变形持续时间**：设置刽子手保持变形多久。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

While shapeshifted, the Hangman kills by strangling. Strangling ignores target statuses such as a Medic shield, Bodyguard protection, or Superstar abilities. It leaves no body and does not trigger victim abilities (for example, Veteran retaliation or a Lucky player surviving); Seer receives no alert.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Hangmen in a match.
- **Shapeshift Cooldown**: Time before the Hangman can shapeshift to use the ability again.
- **Shapeshift Duration**: How long the Hangman remains shifted.
