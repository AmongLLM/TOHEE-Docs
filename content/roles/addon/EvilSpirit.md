## 效果

灵魂召唤者可将符合条件的目标转化为恶灵。该身份不会随机分配。恶灵死亡时会变为守护天使；使用守护能力可短暂定身目标、阻止其报告尸体，或暂时降低其视野。若恶灵选择灵魂召唤者为守护目标，则会在设定时长内保护灵魂召唤者。灵魂召唤者获胜时，恶灵也会获胜。

## 相关设置

- **Spiritcaller ability settings（灵魂召唤者能力设置）**：灵魂召唤者设置控制恶灵数量、恶灵能力冷却、对灵魂召唤者的保护时间、定身时长以及视野降低幅度和持续时间。
- **随机分配**：不会开局随机分配；由上述赋予者能力转化获得。

<!-- en -->

## Effect

Spiritcaller can convert eligible targets into Evil Spirits. This identity is not randomly assigned. When an Evil Spirit dies, it becomes a Guardian Angel; using its protect ability can briefly freeze a target, prevent them from reporting bodies, or reduce their vision. If it protects Spiritcaller, it grants Spiritcaller temporary protection. Evil Spirits win when Spiritcaller wins.

## Related settings

- **Spiritcaller ability settings**: Spiritcaller settings control the number of Evil Spirits, their ability cooldown, Spiritcaller protection duration, freeze duration, and the amount and duration of vision reduction.
- **Random assignment**: Not assigned at random at game start; it is granted by the conversion ability described above.
