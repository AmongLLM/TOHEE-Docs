## 神

神从游戏开始就知道所有玩家的职业。普通游戏结束时若神仍存活，神会取代主要获胜阵营；符合额外胜利条件的玩家仍可能一同获胜。神不会成为恋人。

### 设置

- **人数上限**：一局中可出现的神的数量上限。
- **会议公布神仍存活**：开启后，只要神存活，会议中就会公布神仍在场。
- **可在猜测模式或拥有猜测者附加职业时猜测**：设置神能否参与猜测。

<!-- en -->
## God

God knows every player's role from the beginning. If alive when a normal game end is resolved, God takes the primary win; players who meet separate additional-win conditions may also win. God cannot become a Lover.

### Settings

- **Maximum number:** Maximum number of Gods in a match.
- **Announce God is alive at meetings:** When enabled, meetings announce that God is still alive.
- **Can guess:** Whether God can use role guessing.
