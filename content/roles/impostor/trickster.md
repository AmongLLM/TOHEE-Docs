## 技能

骗术师按普通内鬼方式行动，但船员阵营判定会将其视为船员。警长不能击杀骗术师，灵媒不会将其视为邪恶，告密者无法找到；法官或邪恶法官审判骗术师时会自杀。

## 选项

- **数量上限（Max）**：设置本局最多可有多少名骗术师。

<!-- en -->
## Ability

The Trickster otherwise acts like a regular Impostor, but Crewmate-team checks treat it as a Crewmate. Sheriff cannot kill it, Psychic does not see it as evil, and Snitch cannot find it. A Judge or Councillor who trials the Trickster dies.

## Options

- **Max**: Maximum number of Tricksters in a match.
