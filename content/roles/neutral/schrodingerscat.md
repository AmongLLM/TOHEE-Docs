## 薛定谔的猫

首次有人通过普通击杀按钮攻击薛定谔的猫时，猫会记录攻击者并阻止这次攻击。猫不会变更角色或阵营，仍计为无阵营；若记录的攻击者成为获胜者，猫会作为额外获胜者一同获胜。该保护只会触发一次。

任务执行者的炸弹和杀戮机器的强制击杀会绕过这次保护，因此仍可能杀死猫。

## 设置

- **人数上限**：一局中可出现的薛定谔的猫数量上限。

<!-- en -->
## Schrodinger's Cat

The first ordinary kill-button attack on the Cat records the attacker and is blocked. The Cat does not change role or team and continues to count as no team. If the recorded attacker wins, the Cat wins alongside them as an additional winner. This protection happens only once.

Taskinator bombs and Killing Machine's forced kill bypass this protection and can still kill the Cat.

## Settings

- **Maximum number:** Maximum number of Schrodinger's Cats in a match.
